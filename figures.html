Abstract Figures & Sequences Time-Trial

Analyze 22 randomized visual pattern sequences under time-trial conditions. Identify the underlying logical transformation and select the missing figure (1–5).

Test Instructions

  1. Examine the top row of frames from left to right to determine the sequential rule (rotations, reflections, element counts, additions/subtractions, or alternating steps).
  2. Identify which of the 5 numbered figures (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) in the bottom row logically replaces the ? symbol.
  3. All 22 sequences (a through v) are shuffled randomly on every run.
  4. Work quickly and accurately — both total completion time and percentage accuracy are recorded.

Controls & Shortcuts

  3. Review Navigation: Jump directly to any question using the numbered pills in the bottom bar.
  4. Submit: Click END at any time to finalize the test and review detailed explanations.
