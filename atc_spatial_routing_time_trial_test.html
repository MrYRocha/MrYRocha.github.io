Air Traffic Control Routing Time-Trial

Analyze 12 randomized airspace scenarios under time-trial conditions. Determine which aircraft has first priority and select the valid route from the 4 choices.

Flight Instructions

  1. An airborne aircraft's route must begin at the reporting point closest to the aircraft's center location.
  2. The route must traverse only through reporting points and finish at the point closest to the start of the active runway (L, C, B, G).
  3. Crossing over the runways is prohibited.
  4. After takeoff, a route must pass the point nearest the end of the runway and continue to the requested departure point.

Priority Hierarchy

  1. Airborne vs. Ground: An airborne aircraft (3-digit speed) always has priority over an aircraft on the ground (2-digit taxi speed inside the gray airport zone).
  2. Speed: The faster the aircraft's speed (knots), the higher its priority.
  3. Tie-Breaker: When speeds are identical, choose the aircraft with the shortest route to the runway in use.
  4. Messages: Active restrictions, emergencies, or closed areas in the MESSAGE box override default rules.
